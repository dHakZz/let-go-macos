# Privacy & Safety

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Let Go is designed to inspect your Mac locally and only when you ask it to.

## File inspection

- File, folder, drive, process, and path information stays on your Mac.
- Let Go does not upload the files you inspect, their contents, their paths, or inspection results.
- Let Go does not scan continuously in the background.
- Let Go does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Suggestions

When you deliberately press **Send Suggestion**, the name, email address, subject, suggestion text, app version, and macOS version shown in the form are sent to FormSubmit for delivery to the developer. FormSubmit states that submissions may be retained for up to 30 days. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information in a suggestion.

## Process actions

Let Go can send a normal quit request to a process owned by the current user. It does not force-quit system processes. Before acting, the app shows the process it found and asks the user to confirm potentially disruptive actions.

## External links

The Privacy & Safety, Suggestions, and optional support links open only when selected by the user. Their destination websites may have their own privacy practices.
